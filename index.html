How the numbers were made

  • Sales are net of discounts and before sales tax, tips and shipping. Refunds are subtracted in the month they appear in the export.
  • Orders counts each receipt, online order or invoice once, however many items it has.
  • Duplicates are lines that appear twice in the same system's exports (for example two downloads with overlapping dates); 337 were removed.
  • 7 rows were left out because they were not sales: cancelled or unpaid orders, total rows, or rows without a date or amount. Each one is listed by file below.
  • Shopify's order export shows one refunded total per order and no refund date, so Shopify refunds are counted on the order date and capped at the order's product value.
  • $408.60 of sales has no category. Add rules to a categories file to sort these lines.
